One funny single-line one-star review written by a house cat about its
human, for the Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human bounty on
make.fun. The cat gives one star, notes the human fills the bowl at 6am,
then objects that the human sleeps through the cat's 3am performance
review. The deadpan review shape holds, the complaint is specific (the
food bowl, the 3am wake-up call), and the sting lands in the last few
words — the review gives away more about the cat, who genuinely believes
it is the boss, than about the owner.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the review to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–200 characters).
